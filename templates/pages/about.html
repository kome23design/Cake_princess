Our Story

Crafting Sweet Legacies in Yaounde.

Founded in the heart of Yaounde, Cake Princess began with a simple mission: to bring world-class pastry art and gourmet dining to Cameroon. What started as a small boutique bakery has evolved into a premier destination for those who seek elegance in every bite.

Our team of master pastry chefs and culinary experts work tirelessly to blend traditional African flavors with modern European techniques, ensuring that every cake, pastry, and meal is a masterpiece of taste and design.

The Journey

A Decade of Culinary Art

From our humble beginnings as a small boutique bakery, we've always believed that food is an expression of love and art. Every recipe in our kitchen has a story, passed down through generations and refined by modern culinary techniques.

Today, Cake Princess stands as a symbol of luxury and quality in Yaounde, serving thousands of happy guests who share our passion for the finer things in life.
