Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services in Yaounde.

How do I place an order for delivery?

+
You can place an order directly through our website by adding items to your cart and proceeding to checkout. Alternatively, you can contact us via WhatsApp for a more personalized ordering experience.

Which areas in Yaounde do you deliver to?

+
We deliver to all major neighborhoods in Yaounde, including Jouvance, Bastos, Odza, Mendong, and Ngousso. Delivery fees may vary slightly depending on your distance from our kitchen opposite Total Jouvance.

How far in advance should I book a surprise event?

+
For engagement and birthday surprises, we recommend booking at least 3-5 days in advance to allow our decor team to prepare everything perfectly. For simple table reservations, 24 hours is usually sufficient.

Do you accept Mobile Money payments?

+
Yes, we accept both Orange Money and MTN Mobile Money. You can select this option at checkout, and our delivery agent will verify the transaction upon arrival.
